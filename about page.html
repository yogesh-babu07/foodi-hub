Our Story

FoodieHub started in 2020 in Lucknow with a simple mission - to serve delicious, fresh and affordable food to everyone. What started as a small kitchen is now loved by thousands of customers across Uttar Pradesh.

We believe food is not just about taste, it's about happiness. That's why we use only fresh ingredients, authentic recipes and hygienic cooking methods.



Our Mission

  • Maintain best quality and hygiene
  • Affordable prices for students and families
  • Excellent customer service
